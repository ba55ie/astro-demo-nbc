## Pages, Layouts & Routing

- **File-Based Routing** — Files in `src/pages/` become routes automatically
- **Dynamic Routes** — `[id].astro` becomes `Astro.params.id`
- **Layouts** — Reusable page shells using `<slot />`
- **Server globals** — `Astro.url`, `Astro.redirect('/404')`, etc.

<aside class="notes">
  - Pages and layouts are also Astro Components. - Show in IDE: -
  `src/layouts/DefaultLayout.astro` - `src/pages/posts/index.astro` and
  `src/pages/posts/[id].astro` - `src/pages/photos/[id].astro`
</aside>
