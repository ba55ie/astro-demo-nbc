## Context Over Hype: Takeaways

- **Where Astro Shines**
  - Content-driven websites, e-commerce, docs, marketing, publishing, and blogs
  - Hybrid SSR apps combining fast HTML pages with targeted React/Vue islands
  - Multi-framework teams or gradual migrations
- **When a Full SPA Still Fits**
  - Highly stateful, canvas-heavy, desktop-like web apps (e.g., Figma, Miro, complex real-time dashboards)
