## Why Astro?

- **Server-first & Fast by default** — Zero client JS out of the box
- **Content-focused** — Built for content-rich & hybrid websites
- **UI-agnostic** — React, Preact, Svelte, Vue, Solid & Web Components
- **Flexible** — 100+ integrations (Tailwind, MDX, and more)

<aside class="notes">
  - Reference: https://docs.astro.build/en/concepts/why-astro/ - Move expensive
  hydration off your users' devices. - It should be nearly impossible to build a
  slow website in Astro, and you don't need to throw away your React skills. -
  It started out as a pure SSG (static site generator)
</aside>
