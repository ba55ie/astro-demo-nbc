## Astro Islands Architecture

- **A Sea of Static HTML** — Most of your page ships **0 KB of JS**
- **Islands of Interactivity** — Hydrate only interactive widgets in isolation
- **Server Data → Client Island** — Pass backend data directly via props
- **Multi-Framework** — Mix React, Solid, Vue, etc. on one page

<aside class="notes">
  - Reference: https://docs.astro.build/en/concepts/islands/ - Show in IDE:
  compare `src/pages/components.astro` (SSR only) vs `src/pages/islands.astro`
  (hydrated islands receiving `count` from the server).
</aside>
